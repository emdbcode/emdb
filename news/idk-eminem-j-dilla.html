Eminem and J Dilla: A Collaboration Years in the Making

November 12, 2026

“WELCOME”, a new song from IDK and Eminem is dropping this Friday, and the production credit is certainly worth getting excited about: J Dilla!

The news comes shortly after IDK shared a photograph on social media of himself alongside Eminem in the studio.

Earlier this year, IDK sampled an Eminem skit from the Shady Classics mixtape for “DEViL”, a track from his mixtape album E.T.D.S.. In March, IDK revealed that Eminem had heard the project and was seemingly happy with the sample, writing:

“I just found out today Eminem heard the record and I assume he's not gonna sue me for the little Whoo Kid drop piece with his voice in it. Em said he liked the record.”

But perhaps the most exciting aspect of this announcement is that we'll finally get to hear Eminem over a J Dilla beat. It's a collaboration fans have been hoping to hear for much of Eminem's career, but one that has never quite materialised.

The two Detroit artists have a little history, too. Back in 1996, Eminem reportedly had his hands on the instrumental for A Tribe Called Quest's “Get a Hold”, but couldn't afford the asking price in time. Dilla subsequently gave the beat to Tribe, where it appeared on their album Beats, Rhymes and Life. Dilla also produced a beat for D12 around this period, although Eminem wasn't featured.

So, the question remains: which beat will it be?

When it comes to posthumous releases credited to J Dilla, they most often draw from a catalogue of unfinished productions that have circulated online following his death in 2006.

“WELCOME” - IDK × EMINEM × J DILLA. Friday the 13th, November, 2026.
