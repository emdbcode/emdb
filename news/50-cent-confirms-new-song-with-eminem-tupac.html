50 Cent Confirms New Song with Eminem & 2Pac Coming Soon

July 24, 2026

During an appearance on Today, was asked whether he had a new album on the way. While he didn’t reveal any album plans, he did confirm an unexpected collaboration:

“I have some music that I’m working on — I did something currently — me, Eminem and .”

When the hosts asked whether A.I. had been used to recreate Tupac’s vocals, 50 confirmed that no artificial intelligence was involved. He also revealed that the song was created for the soundtrack of the upcoming Street Fighter movie, in which he plays the role of Balrog.

Tupac was already featured in the film’s official trailer, which used vocals from “Ambitionz Az a Fighta,” a reworked version of “Ambitionz Az a Ridah” created for Mike Tyson. With the trailer already drawing from that recording, it’s highly likely that the upcoming collaboration between 50 Cent, Eminem, and Tupac will be a remix of that same track.

The track will mark Eminem’s first involvement with a Tupac song since 2004, when he produced the posthumous album Loyal to the Game. The new release would also become the third official collaboration between 50 Cent and Tupac, following “The Realest Killaz” from the Tupac: Resurrection soundtrack and the title track from Loyal to the Game.

Street Fighter is scheduled to hit cinemas on October 16, 2026, so the soundtrack could arrive as early as one or two weeks beforehand.

We’ll keep you updated as more information becomes available.
