The Beatles Catalog

The Beatles Catalog is an independent project aiming to bring the history of the Beatles together in one comprehensive, accessible online database.

The aim is to go beyond a conventional discography, bringing together the music itself with detailed album and song information, recording sessions, alternative versions, original editorial content and interactive features to create the ultimate resource for exploring every aspect of The Beatles' music catalogue.

What Sets It Apart?

The Beatles Catalog aims to be the ultimate Beatles rabbit hole.

You'll be able to listen to different versions, mixes and edits of songs directly from the same page, making it easy to compare tracks. Related media, including performances, interviews, promotional films and other material that might otherwise require a separate Google search, will be accessible with a single click. You'll also be able to rate songs and albums, contributing to their overall community scores.

The presentation is just as important as the content. Information is designed to be sleek, concise and easy to navigate, without sacrificing depth or detail. Rather than lengthy blocks of text, information is broken down into clear, digestible sections, delivering the same level of detail in a more accessible format.

Ultimately, it's about bringing everything together in one place and making exploring The Beatles as in-depth and straightforward as possible.

Who's Behind It?

The project is being developed independently by the person behind EMDb, an online database dedicated to Eminem's music career. EMDb was built and is maintained independently, providing the foundation and experience for taking on this new project.

The Beatles Catalog will follow a similar approach, with all research and editorial content being produced independently, without the use of AI.

Why The Beatles?

Quite simply, because I'm a Beatle-maniac, and I know I'm not alone. Thousands, possibly millions, of people would get a kick out of this project.

I'm also from Liverpool, so beyond the music database itself, I want the project to celebrate the city's relationship with The Beatles. With The Beatles: Liverpool 2028 approaching, I'd like to include a dedicated Liverpool section highlighting Beatles-related places, independent businesses, tours and local attractions.

Although the website will be completely ad-free, I hope this section can help support local businesses and the people who continue to keep the city's Beatles heritage alive.

Timeline

The Beatles Catalog is currently planned for launch between late 2027 and early 2028, subject to the project being fully funded by the end of 2026. The design and features may evolve throughout development, with feedback helping to shape the finished site.

Music and Copyright

No music files are hosted or uploaded to The Beatles Catalog. Music is streamed directly from YouTube through a bespoke, custom-coded system developed specifically for the website. Rather than relying on conventional video embeds, the system integrates playback directly into the site's tracklists, creating a more seamless listening experience.
