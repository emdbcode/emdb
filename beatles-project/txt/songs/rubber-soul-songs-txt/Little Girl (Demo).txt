Song Facts

• “Little Girl” is a home demo recorded by John in 1965. It remained completely unknown to the public for six decades, with no bootlegs or even rumours of its existence. Its discovery was first made public when its inclusion on a new <em>Rubber Soul</em> box set was announced in 2026.

• The song was discovered on a Lennon tape reel; Giles Martin edited two sections together to create the 2:17 version featured on Disc 2 of <em>Rubber Soul: Special Edition (Super Deluxe)</em>.

• Many listeners have noted its melodic similarity to the chorus of “I’m Only Sleeping”, recorded the following year and released on <em>Revolver</em>.

Words from Gile's Martin

It was one of those things that came from the Lennon Estate — I think it was Sean. It was one of those things where they say, ‘Oh, and then there’s this.’ [...] Like with all that stuff, I wasn’t sure whether people knew about it or not, or whether it was on YouTube. Quite often I’ll find something and go, ‘This is amazing.’ Then someone goes, ‘Yeah, it’s on YouTube. Everybody knows about that.’ I’m always the last person to know.

— Giles Martin, <em>Rolling Stone</em>, 2026
